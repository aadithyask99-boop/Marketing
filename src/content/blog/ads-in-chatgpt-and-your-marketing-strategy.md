*OpenAI is rolling out advertising to 700 million users. Here is what businesses should be thinking about right now.*

It is official. Ads in ChatGPT are no longer a rumour or a distant possibility. OpenAI has started testing advertising with users in the United States, and the implications for businesses and marketers are significant.

We have been watching this development closely. When a platform with 700 million monthly active users introduces advertising, it changes the landscape. Not gradually. Quickly.

Here is what we know, what it means, and how to think about it practically.

### What Is Actually Happening

OpenAI has begun introducing ads in ChatGPT for US users. According to reports from Wired and [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/01/16/tech/chatgpt-ads-openai), the company is taking a measured approach, testing formats before a wider rollout. This follows months of speculation after OpenAI hired Shivakumar Venkataraman, a senior advertising executive from Google, signalling clear intent.

The reasoning is straightforward. Despite charging up to $200 per month for premium subscriptions and generating billions in API revenue, OpenAI's operating costs are enormous. Running large language models requires massive computing infrastructure. Advertising offers a path to sustainability and profitability that subscriptions alone cannot provide.

For context, ChatGPT now reaches more users than many traditional social media platforms. When ads in ChatGPT become widely available, brands will have access to an audience that is actively seeking information, recommendations, and solutions. That is a different kind of attention than scrolling through a social feed.

### Why This Matters for Businesses

The introduction of ads in ChatGPT represents a shift in how people discover products and services.

Think about how search behaviour has already changed. Millions of people now ask ChatGPT questions they would previously have typed into Google. They ask for product recommendations, service comparisons, travel suggestions, and purchasing advice. If advertising is woven into these conversations, the implications for brand visibility are substantial.

This is not a replacement for existing channels. It is an additional layer. Businesses that rely heavily on search advertising or social media marketing will need to consider how AI platforms fit into their strategy.

The challenge is that we do not yet know exactly how ads in ChatGPT will work at scale. Will they appear as sponsored recommendations within responses? As display ads alongside the chat interface? As affiliate-style links when users ask about products? OpenAI has not revealed full details, and the format will likely evolve based on testing.

What we do know is that ignoring this shift is not a sensible option.

### What Marketers Should Be Thinking About

Rather than rushing to react, this is a moment for strategic thinking. Here are the questions worth considering.

**How does your audience use AI tools?**

If your customers are already using ChatGPT for research and recommendations in your industry, ads in ChatGPT could become a direct channel to reach them. Understanding current behaviour helps you anticipate where to invest.

**Is your content AI-friendly?**

AI systems learn from and reference online content. Brands with clear, well-structured, and authoritative content are more likely to be surfaced in AI-generated responses, whether through organic mentions or paid placements. Your content strategy matters more than ever.

**How flexible is your advertising budget?**

New platforms often offer lower costs and less competition in their early stages. Businesses that test early can learn what works before prices rise and competition intensifies. This does not mean shifting budget immediately, but having flexibility to experiment is valuable.

**Are you tracking the right metrics?**

As advertising expands to AI platforms, measurement will become more complex. Attribution models built for search and social may not apply directly. Prepare for a period of learning and adaptation.

### How to Prepare Now

You do not need to overhaul your marketing strategy today. But you should be laying groundwork.

Stay informed. Follow developments as OpenAI expands ads in ChatGPT beyond initial testing. The rollout to markets outside the US will follow, and details about ad formats, targeting, and pricing will emerge.

Audit your digital presence. Ensure your website, content, and data are in good shape. AI systems favour clear, structured, and trustworthy information. This benefits you whether or not you advertise directly.

Strengthen your first-party data. As advertising platforms evolve and privacy regulations tighten, your own customer data becomes increasingly valuable. Invest in collecting and using it effectively.

Work with partners who understand the landscape. The pace of change in digital marketing is accelerating. Having advisors who track these shifts and help you respond strategically is more important than trying to monitor everything yourself.
