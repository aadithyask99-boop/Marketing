ChatGPT Ads are now available to advertisers in India, giving businesses a new way to reach people while they research products, [compare options and make decisions.](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/)

On 31 August 2026, OpenAI announced that self-service access to ChatGPT Ads was expanding to India, alongside markets across Europe, the Middle East and North Africa. Businesses can now use Ads Manager to create and manage campaigns directly.

For businesses already investing in Google, Meta or LinkedIn advertising, ChatGPT Ads in India offer a different advertising environment. Instead of appearing alongside a traditional list of search results, ads can appear within a conversation where someone is actively exploring a question or considering their options.

So, what are ChatGPT Ads, how do they work in India, and how can a business get started?

## What are ChatGPT Ads?

ChatGPT Ads are paid advertisements displayed within the ChatGPT experience. OpenAI describes them as a way for businesses to reach people as they explore options, compare choices and make decisions.

Ads are displayed separately from ChatGPT's answers and are clearly labelled as sponsored content. [OpenAI also states that advertisers cannot influence or change ChatGPT's responses.](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047)

This distinction matters. A ChatGPT advertisement is not the same as paying ChatGPT to recommend a particular product or company.

The advertising system and the system generating ChatGPT's answers operate separately.

## Are ChatGPT Ads available in India?

Yes. ChatGPT Ads in India are now available through OpenAI Ads Manager.

India was included in OpenAI's global expansion announced on 31 August 2026. The company says self-service access is being introduced across India, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, allowing advertisers to purchase ChatGPT Ads directly.

This makes India one of the markets where businesses can set up campaigns without having to wait for a future launch.

India is also a significant market for ChatGPT itself. OpenAI reported in February 2026 that India had more than 100 million weekly ChatGPT users.

## How to advertise on ChatGPT in India

Businesses can start through OpenAI's Ads Manager. [The platform allows advertisers to create campaigns, add advertising details, launch campaigns and monitor performance.](https://ads.openai.com/)

The basic process involves:

1. Create an Ads Manager account
2. Set up a campaign
3. Choose your campaign objective, budget and dates
4. Set your location and other available targeting options
5. Create your ad groups
6. Add relevant context hints
7. Create your advertising creative
8. Launch and monitor the campaign

One important difference from Google Ads is how relevance is established.

ChatGPT Ads do not simply work by matching an advertisement to an exact keyword. Advertisers can provide context hints that describe the types of conversations or situations where an advertisement may be relevant. OpenAI states that these hints are not exact-match keywords and do not guarantee that an advertisement will appear in a particular conversation.

The system can also consider factors such as conversation context and intent, the landing page, ad copy and other advertiser-provided information.

## How much do ChatGPT Ads cost in India?

The cost of ChatGPT Ads depends on the campaign objective, bidding, competition and other campaign settings.

India currently has a minimum daily campaign budget of ₹725, according to OpenAI's Ads Manager documentation.

Advertisers can currently work with different buying objectives, including cost per thousand impressions, cost per click and optimised cost per click.

The minimum budget should not be confused with the expected cost of generating a customer. A business might be able to launch a campaign with ₹725 per day, but whether that produces profitable results will depend on the offer, audience, advertisement, landing page and conversion rate.

For that reason, businesses should treat an initial campaign as a test. Track the results, identify which messages attract the right visitors and then decide whether to increase spending.

## How are ChatGPT Ads different from Google Ads?

Google Ads and ChatGPT Ads can both help businesses reach people with commercial intent, but they operate in different environments.

With Google, a user generally enters a search query and receives a page of results and advertisements.

With ChatGPT, a user may explain what they are trying to achieve, describe their requirements, ask for comparisons or discuss different options in a conversation.

That creates a different type of advertising opportunity.

For example, someone might search Google for:

"best CRM software for small businesses"

On ChatGPT, that person might ask:

"I run a small business with five employees. Which CRM would be easiest for us to use?"

The second interaction contains considerably more context about the user's situation.

OpenAI describes this richer conversational context as one of the characteristics of advertising in ChatGPT.

ChatGPT Ads should therefore be viewed as another channel alongside established advertising platforms, rather than as a replacement for Google Ads or social advertising.

### What should businesses advertise on ChatGPT?

ChatGPT Ads may be particularly relevant to businesses selling products or services that people research before making a decision.

This could include:

- Software and technology
- E-commerce products
- Professional services
- Education and training
- Travel
- Business services
- Home and lifestyle products
- Financial products where permitted
- Other considered purchases

The important question is not simply whether a business can advertise on ChatGPT. It is whether its customers naturally use conversations to research, compare or evaluate the type of product or service being offered.

## How can businesses create better ChatGPT Ads?

A good ChatGPT advertisement needs to be specific.

Instead of a vague headline such as:

"Take Your Business to the Next Level"

a business could use:

"CRM Software for Growing Small Businesses"

The second example immediately tells the reader what is being offered and who it is for.

OpenAI recommends clear, specific and benefit-focused advertising creative, while also encouraging advertisers to test different variations.

The landing page matters too. If someone clicks an advertisement expecting information about a particular product or service, the landing page should continue that same message rather than sending them to a generic homepage.

This is where advertising, website design and conversion optimisation need to work together.

## Are ChatGPT Ads worth considering for Indian businesses?

It is too early to say that ChatGPT Ads will outperform established advertising channels for every business.

The platform is still developing, and businesses will need to test campaigns and compare their results with other channels.

However, the opportunity is worth watching. ChatGPT is increasingly being used for research, comparisons and decision-making, and OpenAI is now providing businesses with a way to participate in that environment through paid advertising.

For businesses considering ChatGPT Ads in India, the sensible approach is to start with a clearly defined audience, a specific offer, suitable creative and a landing page designed to convert. From there, campaign performance can determine whether the channel deserves a larger share of the advertising budget.

## ChatGPT Ads in India: What happens next?

The arrival of ChatGPT Ads in India gives advertisers another option for reaching potential customers during the research and consideration process.

The channel is new, so there is still plenty to learn about costs, creative formats, targeting and performance across different industries.

Businesses that already have strong digital foundations are likely to be better positioned to test it. A well-structured website, clear positioning, relevant landing pages, reliable conversion tracking and a defined advertising strategy all become important when testing a new channel.
