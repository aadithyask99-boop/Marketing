Google AI Overviews now appear at the top of many search results, summarising answers and citing sources directly. For London businesses, this changes local SEO. If your website is cited, you get visibility before the traditional rankings even begin.

But how do you get your business cited in Google AI Overviews in London when competition is high and thousands of businesses offer similar services?

This guide breaks it down into practical steps you can act on today.

### What Google AI Overviews Cites for London Searches

When someone searches "best accountant in Camden" or "emergency plumber near me" in London, Google AI Overviews pulls information from pages it considers clear, credible, and locally relevant.

The pages that get cited tend to have:

- Direct answers to the question being asked
- Strong local signals such as London service areas, addresses, and reviews
- Structured content that is easy to extract
- Real business details that can be verified

If you want to get your business cited in Google AI Overviews in London, your website needs to look trustworthy and read like a clear answer, not a sales pitch.

### Local Foundations You Need Before Anything Else

Before thinking about how to get your business cited in Google AI Overviews in London, you need to make sure your local SEO basics are solid. Without these foundations, Google has no reason to trust your business for London searches, and AI Overviews will look elsewhere for sources.

Think of these foundations as the minimum requirements. If any of them are weak or inconsistent, your chances of being cited drop significantly, no matter how good your content is.

### Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile helps Google verify your business for local searches and AI Overviews. Keep categories, services, service areas, opening hours, photos, and descriptions accurate. For London businesses, a complete profile improves trust, relevance, and visibility when Google looks for credible local sources to cite.

Choose the right primary and secondary categories

List London service areas clearly

Keep photos, hours, and posts updated

### NAP Consistency

NAP means name, address, and phone number. These details must match exactly across your website, Google Business Profile, directories, and social profiles. Even small differences create doubt. Consistent business details help Google trust your London business and improve your chances of appearing in AI Overviews.

Check website, directories, and social profiles

Fix old addresses and outdated phone numbers

Keep formatting identical everywhere

### Reviews

Reviews are strong local trust signals, especially when they mention the service provided and the London area served. Generic reviews help less. Detailed, genuine reviews give Google more context about your business, improve local relevance, and increase your chances of being cited in AI Overviews.

Ask for reviews after successful projects

Encourage mentions of service and location

Reply to every review professionally

### Contact Page

A strong contact page helps Google verify that your business is real, active, and based in London. Include your business name, address, phone number, email, service areas, and a simple contact form. These trust signals make your website more credible for local search and AI Overviews.

Add full contact details and London address

Include service areas and a map

Use a business email, not Gmail

### Website Changes That Increase Your Chance of Being Cited

This is where most London businesses either win or lose visibility in AI Overviews.

### One Page Per Core Service

If you offer multiple services, give each one a dedicated page. A single page listing everything makes it harder for Google to understand what you specialise in.

Each service page should answer:

- What the service is
- Who it is for
- What areas in London you cover
- What the outcome or benefit is
- What the next step is

### A London Focused Landing Page

Create a page that targets your core London audience without being spammy. Mention the boroughs and areas you serve naturally, not as a keyword list.

Example: "We provide branding and web design services across London, including Camden, Islington, Hackney, Stratford, and Croydon."

### Clear Above the Fold Content

When someone lands on your homepage or service page, they should immediately understand what you do, who you help, and where you operate. Vague headlines lose people and lose citations.

### FAQ Blocks

[Add a short FAQ section to your key pages.](https://ahrefs.com/blog/faq-pages-seo/) Each answer should be 40 to 60 words, direct, and written like a mini answer Google can extract.

Example question: "How long does a website redesign take for London businesses?"

Example answer: "Most website redesigns for London businesses take 4 to 8 weeks depending on the scope. We start with a discovery call, then move into design, development, and testing before launch."

### Proof Near Claims

If you say you are the best, show it. Add testimonials, case studies, before and after examples, or client logos near your claims. AI Overviews favours pages that back up what they say.

### Structured Data and On Page Formatting

[Structured data helps Google understand your content faster.](https://www.woorank.com/en/edu/seo-guides/what-is-structured-data) For local businesses in London, these are the key types to add:

- LocalBusiness schema: includes your name, address, phone, opening hours, and service area
- Service schema: describes each service you offer
- FAQPage schema: marks up your FAQ sections so they can appear in search features
- Review schema: only if reviews are genuine and displayed on the page

You do not need to be a developer to add these. Most website platforms have plugins or built in options.

Beyond schema, format your content so it is easy to scan:

- Use headings that match questions people ask
- Keep paragraphs short
- Use bullet points for lists
- Bold key phrases where it helps readability

### London Authority Signals That Help You Get Cited

Google looks beyond your website to decide if you are a credible London business. Building local authority takes time but pays off.

### Local PR and Features

Get featured in London publications, local blogs, borough business directories, or community news sites. A mention or backlink from a trusted London source signals to Google that your business is relevant and credible for local searches. Start with local chambers of commerce or industry specific London sites.

### Partnerships and Sponsorships

Work with other London businesses, venues, suppliers, or charities and ask for a mention or link on their website. These local connections build trust signals that Google recognises. Sponsor a local event or collaborate on content to earn natural, relevant London backlinks.

### Case Studies With London Clients

Publish case studies that mention the client's industry and London location with permission. A title like "How we helped a Hackney based clinic increase bookings by 40 percent" adds local relevance. Generic testimonials without location details carry far less weight for London searches.

### Quick Checklist

Before you chase AI visibility, get the basics right. Use this checklist to spot gaps in your London local SEO, improve trust signals, and increase your chances of being cited in Google AI Overviews.

- Google Business Profile complete and accurate
- NAP consistent across all platforms
- Reviews mention services and London areas
- One page per core service
- Clear headline and CTA above the fold
- FAQ section with short, direct answers
- LocalBusiness and FAQPage schema added
- At least one London focused landing page
- Case studies or testimonials from London clients
- Local backlinks or mentions from London sites
